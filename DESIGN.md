## Brand & Style

This design system expresses quiet authority, cultural dignity, and radiant optimism. Created for an internationally respected Nigerian NGO, it avoids the patronizing visual clichés often associated with charitable organizations. Instead, it positions the foundation as an institutional catalyst for enduring transformation, community agency, and sustained prosperity.

The aesthetic philosophy balances **Humanist Editorial Craft** with **Modern Warm Tactility**:
- **Dignity & Hope:** Photography and focal imagery showcase strength, resilience, agency, and celebration rather than plight or helplessness. Compositions favor proud eye-level portraits and expansive community vistas.
- **Rooted Sophistication:** Deep botanical greens anchor the system in earthly stewardship and longevity, while sun-drenched ochre and amber tones evoke life, vitality, and dawn.
- **Architectural Breathing Room:** Generous white space rendered in warm limestone and raw sand tones removes clutter, allowing individual stories, impact metrics, and appeals to resonate with clarity.
- **Subtle Cultural Geometry:** UI structures lean into soft organic curves, rounded pill elements, and structured tonal cards that mimic handcrafted earthen architecture.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the Nigerian topography—evergreen agricultural canopies, sunlit Sahelian earth, and natural limestone:

- **Primary (`#134E3F`):** Deep Forest Green. Conveys longevity, growth, institutional stability, and grounding authority. Used for primary CTAs, header text, and structural focal points.
  - *Deep Shade (`#0B352B`):* Reserved for ultra-high-contrast header elements, footer foundations, and inverted content sections.
- **Secondary (`#D97706`):** Warm Radiant Ochre / Gold. Symbolizes vital hope, sunrise, and community agency. Applied to highlight badges, key metric counters, active indicators, and high-priority action points.
  - *Luminous Tones (`#EAB308`, `#F59E0B`):* Used in controlled micro-accents, gradient glows, and progressive stat fills.
- **Tertiary (`#E6EFE9`):** Soft Sage. Acts as an organic bridge, cooling warm imagery and framing data cards without harsh contrast lines.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces:**
  - *Main Canvas (`#FAF8F5`):* Warm unbleached cream that eliminates digital glare and introduces tactile warmth.
  - *Elevated Surface (`#F5F1EB`):* Secondary surface card tint for subtle layering over canvas.
  - *Charcoal Text (`#18221E` / `#232D29`):* Ink-level dark charcoal ensuring AAA accessibility across both cream and light sage backgrounds.

## Typography

The typography system pairs the editorial elegance of **Playfair Display** with the clarity, humanist warmth, and geometric proportions of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

- **Editorial Headings (Playfair Display):** Imparts journalistic rigor, literary credibility, and classical dignity. It conveys that the foundation's initiatives are historic and measured. Headings feature tightened tracking to maintain structural cohesion across larger screen footprints.
- **Humanist Interface & Body (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for high legibility across mobile touchpoints, impact dashboards, long-form community narratives, and financial transparency disclosures. Generous vertical line-heights prevent cognitive fatigue.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Labels leverage uppercase or medium-weighted small-caps with subtle positive letter-spacing (`0.02em` to `0.04em`) to establish crisp hierarchy within badges, chips, and table headers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy implements a **Fluid Adaptive Grid** with generous, disciplined margins. Content breathes deliberately, framing human stories and institutional accountability with weight and clarity.

- **Breakpoints & Layout Models:**
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid grid. Canvas margins: `1.5rem` (`margin`). Gutter: `1rem` (`gutter-sm`). Cards and actionable elements span full width or stacked pairs.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column fluid grid. Canvas margins: `2.5rem` (`margin-md`). Gutter: `1.5rem` (`gutter`).
  - **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column fluid grid with a maximum content bound of `1280px`. Canvas margins: `4rem` (`margin-lg`). Gutter: `2rem` (`gutter-lg`).
- **Rhythm & Structural Grouping:**
  - Use `space-2xl` (`4rem`) for vertical section separation on desktop, decreasing to `space-xl` (`2.5rem`) on mobile.
  - Use `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) within cards and metric containers to create an open, readable layout.
  - Use `space-sm` (`0.5rem`) and `space-md` (`1rem`) for dense contextual connections between icons, titles, and supporting text.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids harsh artificial dropshadows and cold grayscale drop-offs. Visual depth is generated using **Tonal Layering** accompanied by **Warm Ambient Casts**:

1. **Base Ground:** The warm cream canvas (`#FAF8F5`) acts as the natural bottom layer.
2. **Surface Layer (Cards & Panels):** Rendered in `#FFFFFF` or `#F5F1EB`, separated from the base primarily through soft color contrast and subtle ambient tint:
   - *Warm Ambient Elevation:* `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(24, 34, 30, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.03)`.
3. **Elevated Overlays & Modals:** Modals and flyout menus float on top with a deeper, enriched shadow:
   - *Deep Grounded Elevation:* `0 16px 40px -6px rgba(11, 53, 43, 0.12), 0 6px 12px -2px rgba(24, 34, 30, 0.04)`.
4. **Low-Contrast Ghost Borders:** When containers require further definition, they use a soft border tint of `rgba(19, 78, 63, 0.08)` instead of hard grays.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a confident, inviting posture with **Level 2 Roundedness** (`0.5rem` base, scaling to `1rem` and `1.5rem` for medium and large structural containers). 

- **Cards & Primary Modules:** Use `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) and `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px`). This softens content sections, mirroring organic clay and natural forms.
- **Pill Attributes (`rounded-full`):** High-touch elements—such as primary action buttons, status pills, category tags, and search bars—use full pill radii to invite engagement.
- **Images:** Dignified portraiture and community captures use `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem`) corners with subtle inner glow or natural matte framing.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Pill-shaped):** Background `#134E3F`, label in crisp white, typography `label-lg`. Padding: `0.875rem 2rem`. On hover, surface shifts to `#0B352B` with a subtle transform lift (`translate-y: -1px`).
- **Empowerment / Secondary CTA:** Background `#D97706`, text `#FFFFFF`. Used for high-impact actions like "Donate Now" or "Sponsor an Initiative." Hover shifts to `#B45309`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary Button:** Border `1.5px solid #134E3F`, transparent background, text `#134E3F`. Hover background blends with `rgba(19, 78, 63, 0.05)`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Impact & Region Tags:** Pill form factor (`rounded-full`), padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`.
- **Style Variants:**
  - *Forest Soft:* `#134E3F` text over `#E6EFE9` background.
  - *Vitality Amber:* `#B45309` text over `#FEF3C7` background.
  - *Neutral Outline:* `rgba(24, 34, 30, 0.1)` border with dark charcoal text.

### Cards
- **Impact Story Cards:** White (`#FFFFFF`) or pale sand (`#F5F1EB`) backgrounds, radius `rounded-xl`, subtle border `rgba(19, 78, 63, 0.06)`, padded by `space-lg`. Imagery sits flush at top or alongside with internal corner radii.
- **Metric Highlight Cards:** Elevated with a warm ambient cast, large numerical values rendered in `headline-xl` (Playfair Display, `#134E3F` or `#D97706`), paired with micro-labels in uppercase `label-md`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Text Inputs & Dropdowns:** Minimum touch target height `48px`. Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid rgba(24, 34, 30, 0.16)`, radius `0.5rem` (`rounded-md`). Text `#18221E`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts cleanly to `#134E3F` with an outer glow of `0 0 0 3px rgba(19, 78, 63, 0.15)`.
- **Labels:** Positioned outside above inputs, typography `label-md` in `#18221E`.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Checkboxes:** Rounded squares (`0.25rem`), `20px × 20px`. Inactive: `1.5px solid rgba(24, 34, 30, 0.25)`. Checked: `#134E3F` fill with a sharp white checkmark icon.
- **Radios:** Circular `20px × 20px`. Active state displays a central `#134E3F` dot with `3px` white space inside a deep green outline.

### Lists & Financial Transparency Rows
- **List Items:** Separated by soft hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(19, 78, 63, 0.08)`).
- **Audit & Governance Rows:** Clean horizontal alignment pairing date/category tags on the left, clear descriptive narrative in `body-md`, and download/verification triggers on the right.
